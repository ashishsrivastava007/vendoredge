AI / Decision Support Disclaimer

Last updated: [date]

1. What VendorEdge is

VendorEdge uses AI (large language models) to help analyze commercial questions — such as supplier price increases and quote comparisons — and to generate a suggested commercial position, along with the reasoning, assumptions, and confidence behind it.

2. What VendorEdge is not

VendorEdge is a decision-support tool. It is not legal, financial, tax, accounting, or procurement-certification advice, and it does not replace the professional judgment of qualified advisors or your own organization's approval processes. No output from VendorEdge should be treated as a guarantee of any particular commercial or financial outcome.

3. AI-generated content can be wrong

Recommendations are generated by an AI model based on the information you provide. That output may be incomplete, may rest on incorrect assumptions, or may not account for information you did not enter. Every recommendation includes the specific assumption it depends on and the condition that would change it — please read and verify both before acting.

4. Confidence levels are not accuracy guarantees

The "confidence" shown with each recommendation reflects how much supporting evidence the system had available, not an independently verified probability of correctness.

5. Organizational history

VendorEdge may reference your organization's own previously recorded outcomes when generating new recommendations. This history is only as accurate and complete as what your organization's users have entered, is not independently verified, and is not shared with or drawn from any other organization.

6. Your responsibility

The final commercial decision, and responsibility for it, always rests with you and your organization — not with VendorEdge. Please apply your own judgment, and your organization's normal review and approval processes, before acting on any recommendation.
